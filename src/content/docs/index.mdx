## Free, open and in your language

ekPanel is free software under the [AGPL-3.0 licence](/project/license/). **ek** (এক) is Bangla
for "one": one panel for your whole hosting. Every screen is in English and বাংলা from day one,
and the panel is built so more languages can follow. [Help translate](/development/translating/).
