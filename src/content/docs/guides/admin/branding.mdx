**Branding**, also called **white-label**, gives the panel your own name, color, logo and domain,
so your customers see your hosting business instead of ekPanel. An administrator sets the
panel's brand for everyone. A [reseller](/guides/admin/resellers/) an administrator allows can
set their own, which they and their customers see.

<Aside type="caution" title="New after v0.1.0">
Branding is in the main repository's `dev` branch and comes with the **next release**. A panel on
v0.1.0 does not have the **Branding** page yet.
</Aside>

## Before you start

- **Administrators:** **Branding** is under **Administration** in the menu, above **Updates**
  (the page is `/settings/branding`). You can also open it from the command palette (`Ctrl K`).
- **Resellers:** **Branding** is under **Reseller** in the menu, below **Customers** and
  **Packages**. An administrator must first [allow you your own branding](#let-a-reseller-use-their-own-brand);
  until then the page says "Branding is not turned on for you" and nothing on it can be changed.
- **For a domain:** a DNS name you control, such as `panel.example.com`, and the server that
  runs the panel [added to the panel](/guides/admin/servers/), with ports 80 and 443 open.
  The panel's own server is what answers the domain.

## Who sees which brand

| Who | Sees |
| --- | --- |
| Administrators, your own customers, and resellers without their own branding and their customers | The panel's brand |
| A reseller with their own branding, and all their customers | The reseller's brand |
| An administrator who used **Sign in as** on a reseller or a customer | That account's brand |
| Someone not signed in yet (the sign-in page) | The brand of the domain they opened: a reseller's domain shows the reseller's brand, any other address the panel's |

Once signed in, the brand follows the **account**, not the address: a reseller's customer who
signs in at the panel's main address still sees the reseller's brand.

With nothing set, the panel looks like ekPanel.

## Set the panel's brand

As an administrator:

<Steps>

1. Open **Branding**.

2. Enter a **Name**, up to 60 characters. Leave it empty to keep the name ekPanel.

3. Choose a **Color** with the color picker, or type it as `#rrggbb`, such as `#1a7f4b`.
   Leave it empty, or click **Use the default**, for ekPanel's green.

4. Enter a **Domain** if the panel should answer at its own address, such as
   `panel.example.com` (see [Domain](#domain)), or leave it empty.

5. Click **Save**. The page says "Saved".

6. Under **Logo**, click **Upload a logo** and choose a picture (see [Logo](#logo)).

</Steps>

**Preview** shows the name or logo, a button, a link and a highlight in your color, adjusted as the
panel will use it, in the light and the dark
theme. Saving applies it everywhere.

## Let a reseller use their own brand

A reseller cannot set a brand until an administrator allows it.

<Steps>

1. Open **Resellers** and click **Edit reseller** on the reseller's card.

2. Tick **Own branding**: "The reseller can give the panel their own name, logo, color and
   domain for their customers." It is off for new resellers.

3. Click **Save changes**.

</Steps>

The reseller's card then shows an <Badge text="Own branding" /> badge.

Untick it to take it back. The reseller and their customers then see the panel's brand again,
and the reseller's domain is no longer served. Their settings are kept, so ticking it again
brings everything back.

## Set a reseller's brand

As a reseller, once an administrator has allowed it:

<Steps>

1. Open **Branding**: "Your own name, logo, color and domain. You and your customers see the
   panel this way."

2. Enter a **Name**. A reseller must give one.

3. Choose a **Color**, or leave it empty for the panel's color.

4. Enter a **Domain** if you like: "Your customers sign in here and see your branding even before
   signing in. Links in their emails use it too." Leave it empty to use the panel's.

5. Click **Save**.

6. Under **Logo**, click **Upload a logo**. You must save a name first: "Save your panel's name
   first."

</Steps>

A reseller's name and logo go together. Without a logo of your own, your customers see your name
with a tile of its first letter, **not** the panel's logo. The color and the domain fall back to
the panel's when you leave them empty.

## Name

The name is shown:

- in the header and on the sign-in page, next to a tile of its first letter when there is no logo;
- in the browser tab's title;
- in [notifications](/guides/admin/notifications/), such as "Someone signed in to your Host BD
  account from …" and "Open in Host BD: …";
- in the panel's texts that used to say ekPanel, such as "Host BD creates a strong password and
  shows it once.";
- in authenticator apps, as the account's name, when someone turns on
  [two-factor sign-in](/guides/security/accounts/#two-factor-sign-in). Codes set up before the
  name changed keep the old name in the app; they still work.

It can be up to 60 characters, without `<` or `>`.

## Color

The color is used for buttons, links and highlights. "It is made darker or lighter where needed,
so text stays readable in light and dark themes": the panel adjusts it so text keeps a contrast
of at least 4.5:1 in both themes. The colors that show a state (green, amber and red, such as
**Active** or **Failed**) do not change.

## Logo

- PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 2 MB, at least 16×16 pixels. SVG is not accepted.
- The panel reads the picture and saves it again as a PNG, scaled down to fit 1024×512 pixels,
  so only plain pixels are ever served.
- It is shown about 32 pixels high in the header and on the sign-in page, and is also the browser
  tab's icon. "A wide logo with a transparent background reads best in both themes."

Click **Change the logo** to upload another, or **Remove** to remove it.

## Domain

A brand's domain is an extra address for the panel, such as `panel.example.com`. The server that
runs the panel answers it: its Caddy gets a free Let's Encrypt certificate once the domain's DNS
points at that server, and passes the requests on to the panel. Visitors who are not signed in see
that brand's sign-in page there.

A domain is claimed like a website's: no website, app or other brand can use it at the same time.
If one does, saving says "A website, an app or another panel already uses this domain."

<Steps>

1. Enter the **Domain** and click **Save**.

2. The page lists the DNS records to make: "Point panel.example.com at the panel's server with
   these DNS records:", an `A` record with the server's public IPv4 address and an `AAAA` record
   with its IPv6 address, each with a copy button.

3. Make those records where the domain's DNS is hosted.

4. Wait until the badge next to the domain shows **Active**, then open the domain.

</Steps>

The badge shows the domain's state:

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | The panel's server serves the domain. Its certificate comes once the domain's DNS points at the server. |
| **Being set up** | The panel's server has not taken it up yet. The page checks again every few seconds. |
| **Failed** | The panel's server could not set it up. Administrators see the error under the domain. |
| **No server** | "The server that runs the panel is not added to the panel, so nothing can answer this domain yet." [Add the server](/guides/admin/servers/) first. |

Some domains are treated differently:

- Domains ending in `.test`, `.localhost`, `.internal`, `.example` or `.invalid` get a certificate
  from Caddy's own authority instead of Let's Encrypt, for labs and tests. Browsers warn about it.
- A reseller cannot use the panel's own address: the one they have the panel open at, or the
  **Panel address** in the [notification settings](/guides/admin/notifications/#before-you-start-the-mail-server-administrators).
- A domain you set up with the installer's `--domain` is served by the server's main Caddyfile
  already, so the panel does not add it a second time. That address always shows the panel's
  brand.

Links in a user's notifications use their brand's domain (`https://` and the domain), or else the
**Panel address** from the notification settings.

### Passkeys and more than one address

"Passkeys belong to one address: a passkey added at another of the panel's addresses does not
work here." A [passkey](/guides/security/accounts/#passkeys) added at `panel.example.com` does not
work at `cp.example.net`. To use passkeys at a new address, sign in there with your password and
two-factor code, and add a passkey there as well.

## What stays ekPanel

- **The source code link.** A small **Source code** link stays at the bottom of the menu and of
  the sign-in page. The panel's license, the [AGPL](/project/license/), asks it to offer its source
  code to everyone who uses it, so it cannot be turned off.
- **Texts about the software itself** keep the name ekPanel: [updates](/guides/admin/updates/)
  ("ekPanel 1.2 is available"), "the ekPanel agent" in the firewall's texts, and the setup page.
- **The "Website suspended" and "Bandwidth used up" pages** that websites show are plain pages
  served by the web server, without any brand.

## With the API

| Endpoint | What for | Token scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/branding` | The brand for the caller when signed in, or for the domain otherwise: `name`, `color`, `logo_url`, `custom`, `url`, `source_url`. Public | none |
| `GET /api/v1/branding/logo/{id}` | A logo, as a PNG. Public | none |
| `GET /api/v1/brand` | Your settings: for an administrator the panel's, for a reseller their own, with `allowed`, `domain_state`, `addresses` and `panel` (the panel's brand they fall back to) | `settings` (or `read`) |
| `PUT /api/v1/brand` | Save `{"name": "…", "color": "…", "domain": "…"}` | `settings` |
| `PUT /api/v1/brand/logo` | Upload a logo: the picture is the body, sent as `application/octet-stream` | `settings` |
| `DELETE /api/v1/brand/logo` | Remove the logo | `settings` |

For example, with a token that has the **Settings** scope:

```bash
export EKP_TOKEN='ekp_…'   # from Your account → API tokens
PANEL=https://panel.example.com/api/v1

curl -sS -X PUT -H "Authorization: Bearer $EKP_TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"name":"Host BD","color":"#1a7f4b","domain":""}' \
  "$PANEL/brand"

curl -sS -X PUT -H "Authorization: Bearer $EKP_TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/octet-stream' \
  --data-binary @logo.png "$PANEL/brand/logo"
```

`PUT /brand` saves all three fields at once, so send the ones you want to keep too. An
administrator allows a reseller their own branding with `"white_label": true` in the reseller's
limits (`POST` or `PUT /resellers`). See the [API reference](/reference/api/#administrators-and-resellers).

The audit log records "Branding changed", "Logo changed" and "Logo removed"
(`brand.updated`, `brand.logo_changed`, `brand.logo_removed`).

## Troubleshooting

| Message or problem | What it means and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Enter a name of up to 60 characters, without < or >." (`invalid_brand_name`) | The name is too long or has `<` or `>`. A reseller must also give a name. |
| "Enter a color such as #1a7f4b." (`invalid_color`) | Type the color as `#` and six hexadecimal digits, or leave it empty. |
| "Upload a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP picture of at least 16×16 pixels." (`invalid_logo`) | The file is not one of those formats (SVG is not accepted), or it is too small. |
| "The logo can be up to 2 MB." (`logo_too_large`) | Make the file smaller. |
| "Save your panel's name first." (`brand_name_first`) | A reseller must save a name before uploading a logo. |
| "A website, an app or another panel already uses this domain." (`domain_taken`) | Another website, app or brand has it, or (for a reseller) it is the panel's own address. Choose another domain. |
| "An administrator has not allowed you your own branding." (`white_label_off`) | Ask an administrator to tick **Own branding** for you. |
| The domain does not answer, or has no valid certificate | Check that its `A` and `AAAA` records point at the addresses the **Branding** page lists, and that ports 80 and 443 reach the panel's server. See [The domain of a brand does not answer](/reference/troubleshooting/#the-domain-of-a-brand-does-not-answer). |
| My passkey does not work at the new domain | Passkeys belong to one address. Sign in with your password and two-factor code, and add a passkey at this address too. |

## Limits and what is not there yet

- One brand for the panel and one for each reseller; a reseller's customers cannot have one of
  their own.
- Each brand has one domain.
- The emails' sender address is the one in the notification settings, for every brand.
- The "Website suspended" and "Bandwidth used up" pages carry no brand.
- **Sub-users** are not there yet: they are the next part of phase 4e on the [roadmap](/project/roadmap/).
