ekPanel is free software under the
[GNU Affero General Public License, version 3](https://github.com/ekPanel/ekpanel/blob/dev/LICENSE)
(`AGPL-3.0-only`). This page explains what that means in practice.

<Aside type="note">
This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. The licence text itself is what counts.
</Aside>

## What you may do

- **Use it** for any purpose, including commercial hosting for paying customers.
- **Study** how it works: all the source code is there.
- **Change** it to fit your needs.
- **Share** it, changed or not, with anyone.

You do not pay anything, and you do not need to ask anyone.

## What you must do

The AGPL adds duties only in two situations:

| You… | Then you must |
| --- | --- |
| **give copies** of ekPanel to others (changed or not), for example as a download | Give them the source code too, under the same licence, with the licence and copyright notices kept. |
| **run a changed version** as a service that other people use over a network, for example your customers sign in to your modified panel | Offer those users the source code of your changed version, under the same licence. |

If you run ekPanel **unchanged**, even for thousands of customers, you have no extra duties.
Changes you keep for yourself and do not let others use are your own business too.

That second rule is what makes the AGPL different from the GPL. It is why the project chose it:
companies that offer ekPanel as a hosted service must share their improvements, instead of
keeping a closed fork. Licences such as MIT or Apache-2.0 would allow closed forks.

### "Only"

`AGPL-3.0-only` means version 3 of the AGPL, and not automatically any later version.

### Your contributions

By opening a pull request to the main repository, you agree that your contribution is released
under the same licence, `AGPL-3.0-only`. See [Contributing](/development/contributing/).

## Dependencies

The panel and agent are built with libraries under licences that are compatible with the AGPL:

- **Go libraries**: MIT (chi, pgx, embedded-postgres, pty), BSD (Go's `x/crypto` and `x/net`,
  the crypt library), ISC (the WebSocket library) and Apache-2.0 (the TOTP library).
- **Web UI**: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, TanStack Query, i18next, CodeMirror, xterm.js and lucide
  icons, under permissive licences such as MIT.
- **Fonts**: Noto Sans, Noto Sans Bengali and JetBrains Mono, under the SIL Open Font License,
  self-hosted inside the panel.

### Programs ekPanel installs on your servers

The agent installs separate programs on your servers when you use a feature: Caddy, PostgreSQL,
MariaDB, PHP, Docker, Stalwart, Roundcube, PowerDNS, CrowdSec, ClamAV, Pure-FTPd, restic,
WP-CLI, Railpack and others. They are not part of ekPanel's code; each comes from its own
project, under its own licence. The same is true for the apps in the catalogue (Nextcloud, Ghost,
Grafana…), whose container images come from their publishers.

## Country data (DB-IP)

Country blocking uses the free country list from
[DB-IP](https://db-ip.com) ("IP to Country Lite"). It is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence, which
requires credit. The panel shows **IP Geolocation by DB-IP** with a link where the data is used.
The list is downloaded to each server that uses it, once a month; it is not shipped inside
ekPanel. If you change the panel, keep that credit.

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): you may share and adapt it for any
purpose, as long as you give appropriate credit. The site's code (configuration and styles) is
under the MIT licence.

## Name and logo

The logo files are in the main repository under `assets/brand/`, with rules for using them in
`assets/brand/README.md`. In short:

- Write the name **ekPanel**: small e, capital P.
- Do not stretch or squash the logo, and do not change the colour of the tile or the green
  cursor.
- Keep free space around it of at least half the icon's height.
- On photos or patterns, use the one-colour or reversed version.

There is no written trademark policy yet. If you want to use the name or logo for your own
product, service or fork, please ask first at ekpanelbd@gmail.com.
