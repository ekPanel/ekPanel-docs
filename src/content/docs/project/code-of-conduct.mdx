ekPanel is built by people from many countries, in many languages, with every level of
experience. We want taking part to be a good experience for all of them. This code says what we
expect from everyone, and what to do when something goes wrong.

Our code is based on the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The binding text is
[`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](https://github.com/ekPanel/.github/blob/HEAD/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in the
organisation's `.github` repository; this page explains it in plain words.

## Where it applies

Everywhere ekPanel happens: every ekPanel repository, issue, pull request, discussion, chat and
event, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Our promise

Taking part is free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, social and economic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient**, especially with people who are new, and with people writing in a
  language that is not their first. Many of us write in English as a second or third language.
- **Respect different opinions and experiences.** Disagree about ideas, never about people.
- **Give and accept feedback gracefully.** Say sorry when you got something wrong, and learn
  from it.
- **Think about the whole community**, not only about yourself.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insults, put-downs, and personal or political attacks
- Harassment, in public or in private
- Publishing other people's private information, such as their address or email, without their
  explicit permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, write to **ekpanelbd@gmail.com**. Every report is read and
handled privately, and we protect the privacy of the person who reports.

It helps to include what happened, where (a link if there is one), when, and who was involved.
You do not need to be sure that it breaks this code; if it worries you, tell us.

<Aside type="note">
For a security problem in ekPanel's code, see the [security policy](/project/security-policy/)
instead.
</Aside>

## What happens next

The maintainers decide what to do, guided by the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
That can range from a private word, to a warning, to a temporary or permanent ban from the
project's spaces.
